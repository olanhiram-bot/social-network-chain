# Design — Nexo

## Genre

Modern-minimal product workspace.

## Macrostructure family

- App pages: Workbench — a focused content column, persistent navigation, and contextual utility rail.
- Content surfaces: Ecosystem index — mixed feed cards, discovery cards, and conversation panels.

## Theme

- Paper: cool near-white with ink-blue contrast.
- Accent: cobalt blue, reserved for active states and primary actions.
- Supporting signal: mint for successful identity and live-status moments.

## Typography

- Display and body: Inter system stack, strong weights only for hierarchy.
- Mono: system monospace for wallets, IDs, and onchain metadata.

## Interaction

- Motion is short and functional: hover lift, focus rings, and panel entrance only.
- Touch targets remain at least 44px on narrow screens.
- Buttons use a compact rounded-rectangle voice; primary actions are cobalt.

## Responsive behaviour

- Desktop: navigation rail, focused feed column, optional context rail.
- Tablet: navigation collapses into the top command row.
- Mobile: single content column with a persistent compact top bar and no horizontal overflow.
